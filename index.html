Who we are

About ACM

The Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) is an international organization dedicated to advancing computing as a science and profession. It connects students, educators, researchers, and professionals to share knowledge, encourage innovation, and explore new technologies.

At SKH ACM, we aim to promote technical learning, collaboration, and innovation through workshops, events, and hands-on projects.

Learn. Collaborate. Innovate.

Our community

About SKH ACM

Building a Community of Future Innovators

SKH ACM is a student-focused computing community at Sapkal Knowledge Hub for students passionate about computer science, technology, programming, and innovation.

Through workshops, coding activities, technical events, and collaborative learning, we help students build practical skills, share ideas, and prepare for future opportunities.

Our Vision

To build an inclusive computing community of skilled developers, creative thinkers, and future technology leaders.

Our Mission

  • Learn together through workshops and hands-on sessions.
  • Build practical projects and meaningful solutions.
  • Collaborate, connect, and grow professionally.
  • Explore emerging technologies and opportunities.

Join ACM

Who Can Join?

Any student interested in computer science, programming, technology, innovation, and learning new skills can join SKH ACM. You don't need prior coding experience — just curiosity, enthusiasm, and a willingness to learn.

What Members Get

  • Access to technical workshops and hands-on sessions.
  • Opportunities to participate in hackathons, coding competitions, and tech events.
  • Collaboration with like-minded students and seniors.
  • Exposure to new technologies, projects, and industry trends.
  • Opportunities to develop leadership, teamwork, and problem-solving skills.

How to Join

  1. Fill Out the Membership Form — Submit your details through the official SKH ACM membership form.
  2. Attend an Orientation — Learn about the club, its activities, upcoming events, and membership opportunities.
  3. Start Participating — Join workshops, contribute to projects, participate in events, and grow with the SKH ACM community.

Questions

FAQ


Who can join SKH ACM?

Any student interested in computer science, technology, programming, or innovation can join SKH ACM. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced developer, you are welcome to learn and grow with us.

Is there a membership fee?

Membership requirements and any applicable fees will be communicated by the SKH ACM team. Students can contact the team for the latest membership details.

Do I need prior coding experience?

No! You don't need prior coding experience. Our activities and learning opportunities are designed to help beginners explore programming and develop their technical skills.

How can I participate in events?

Follow our official announcements and social media updates to stay informed about upcoming workshops, coding competitions, technical sessions, and other events. Register using the link provided for each event.

How can I become a core team member?

Stay active in SKH ACM events, contribute to club activities, and demonstrate teamwork, initiative, and a passion for technology. Core team opportunities and selection details will be announced through official club channels.
